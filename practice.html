The PreCrisis lifecycle

Readiness before response.

This lifecycle is an organizing practice, not a diagnosis or promise of prediction. It helps teams move from better conditions toward proportionate human support.

  1. 01
    Optimize

    Strengthen readiness, resilience, connection, and the conditions in which people operate.

  2. 02
    Detect

    Notice meaningful change while keeping context, uncertainty, and human dignity visible.

  3. 03
    Prevent

    Prepare humane, proportionate support before pressure becomes an emergency.

  4. 04
    Intervene

    Equip an informed, authorized person to decide and act at the responsible moment.
