The operating practice

Intervene earlier.

Evidence before theater

Public claims.
Explicit boundaries.

01

Sites are not source

A public interface can be backed by public, source-available, or private code. Each project is labeled accordingly.

02

Downloads are not people

NPM counts successful package-tarball downloads. Automation, mirrors, scanners, and repeat downloads may contribute.

03

AI supports judgment

TWiN systems are designed to inform accountable human decisions, not erase human discretion or responsibility.

04

Maturity is part of the claim

Released source, development applications, public foundations, private systems, and concepts are labeled as different stages.
